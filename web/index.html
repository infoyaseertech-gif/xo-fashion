About us

Modesty meets comfort, quality and effortless elegance.

At X and O, we believe that looking good while staying modest should always go hand in hand.

We are a Kaduna based modest wear brand dedicated to providing beautiful, quality jilbabs and hijabs that combine comfort, elegance and modesty. Modest fashion is more than what you wear. It is about feeling confident, comfortable and effortlessly beautiful.

Our goal is to make finding the perfect jilbab or hijab an enjoyable and stress free experience, with carefully selected pieces for different tastes and occasions. Whether you are dressing for everyday activities, special occasions or simply embracing your modest style, we are here to make it easier.

Based in Kaduna, we proudly serve customers across Nigeria with convenient nationwide delivery. Welcome to X and O.

Good to know

Delivery, payment and returns

How does delivery work?

We work with a delivery company and ship across Nigeria. The courier fee is paid by you and depends on your state. Message us on WhatsApp for your exact fee and delivery time.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer. After you send your order we reply with our account details. Share your transfer receipt in the chat and we confirm your order.

Will the colour match the photos?

We show each colour on its own photos and videos. Screens vary a little, so ask us for a live photo or video of the exact piece before you pay.

Can I return or exchange something?

Yes. Returns and exchanges are accepted within 2 weeks of delivery.

Are light colours see-through?

Lighter fabrics can look slightly sheer in pale colours. Wear an inner layer, and check the fabric note on each product.

Privacy and terms

Privacy

When you order we collect your name, WhatsApp number, state and delivery address. We use them only to confirm your order, arrange delivery with our courier and answer your questions. We do not sell your details. To see or delete your information, message us on WhatsApp. Your cart is kept on your own device.

Terms of sale

Prices are in naira. An order is confirmed once we receive your bank transfer. Delivery is by our partner courier and the courier fee is paid by the customer. Returns and exchanges are accepted within 2 weeks of delivery. Colours may look slightly different on your screen.
